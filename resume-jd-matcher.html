Methodology & limitations
  • Skills are detected against a curated taxonomy of ~150 common terms (tech, data, design, business, project management, HR, and soft skills), matched by exact word/phrase — not a semantic AI model.
  • Skill coverage = matched skills ÷ skills detected in the job description.
  • Content similarity uses TF-IDF (term frequency × inverse document frequency) cosine similarity between the two full texts. With only two documents being compared, IDF mainly upweights words unique to one side — this is a simplified, illustrative version of a technique that normally runs over a large corpus.
  • Overall match = 65% skill coverage + 35% content similarity.
  • The taxonomy isn't exhaustive and can't judge seniority, tone, or whether a claimed skill is actually strong — treat this as a starting point for tailoring your resume's wording, not a verdict.
